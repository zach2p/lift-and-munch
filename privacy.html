Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

The short version

  • We store your workouts, food logs and body stats so the app can work. That's it.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • Only you can see your data. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Who we are

Lift and Munch is a workout and food tracking app made by Zach Perry, an independent developer in Connecticut. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Lift and Munch, and "you" means anyone who uses the app. If you have a question about this policy or your data, email zach2perry@gmail.com.

Two ways to use the app

Without an account. Everything you enter stays in your web browser on your device. We never receive it. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, it's gone.

With an account. Your information is saved to our database so it syncs across your devices. The rest of this policy mostly covers this case.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Passwords are encrypted by our login provider, and we can never see them.
  • Workouts: your training split, exercises, sets, weights, reps and rest timer settings.
  • Food: your daily food log and the meals you save.
  • Calculator details: the age, sex, height, weight, activity level and goal you enter, and the calorie and macro targets you save.
  • Basic technical data: like most websites, our hosting and service providers may record your IP address, browser type and the time of each request to keep their services secure and working.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or payment details.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your history, autofill weights from past sessions, and track calories against your targets.
  • To sync your data across your devices and keep your account secure.
  • To reply when you contact us.

We don't sell your information, rent it, share it with advertisers, or use it to build ad profiles.

Who we share it with

We only share data with services the app needs to run:

  • Supabase handles sign-in and stores account data. See the Supabase privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's website. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts delivers the app's typefaces, so Google may receive your IP address when the app loads. See the Google privacy policy.

We may also share information if the law requires it, such as a valid court order.

How we protect it

All traffic to the app uses HTTPS encryption. Our database uses access rules so each account can only read and change its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information, and we'll tell you if a breach affects your data.

Your choices and rights

  • See or export your data: email us and we'll send you a copy.
  • Correct it: edit anything in the app, or email us.
  • Delete it: email us from the address on your account and we'll delete your account and all its data within 30 days.
  • Stop using the app: you can stop at any time. Data saved without an account can be removed by clearing your browser's site data.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the Connecticut Data Privacy Act or the GDPR may give you more rights. Email us to use them and we'll respond within 45 days.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your data from our database within 30 days. Copies in our providers' backups may last a little longer before they're removed.

Health information

Body stats, calorie targets and food logs can be sensitive. We use them only to run the app for you and never share them for marketing. Calorie targets, macros and food values are estimates for general fitness use. They aren't medical advice, so talk to a doctor before making big changes to your diet or training.

Children

Lift and Munch isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also let you know in the app or by email before they take effect.

Questions? Email zach2perry@gmail.com.
